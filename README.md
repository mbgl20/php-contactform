## 📌 About

Visitors of your site can hit you up easily by contactform.

---

## ❗ Important Information

For privacy reasons you should NOT have the `/view/index.php` accessable for everyone!

Also the `contact.csv`-file should be in a locked archive like a secured cdn or locked with `.htaccess`!!
